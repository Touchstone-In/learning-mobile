# Play Store Readiness Checklist

## App Listing Metadata

### Basic Information
- **App name:** TSIN Learning Companion
- **Package:** `com.touchstoneinstitute.learningcompanion`
- **Default language:** English (United States)
- **Category:** Education
- **Content rating:** Everyone

### Short Description (80 chars max)
> Training schedule, notifications, and learning progress for TSIN learners.

### Full Description (4000 chars max)
> TSIN Learning Companion is the official mobile app for learners enrolled in Touchstone Institute training programs. Stay on top of your training schedule, receive timely reminders for upcoming sessions and orientation events, and track your learning progress — all from your phone.
>
> Features:
> - View your personalized training schedule
> - Receive push notifications for schedule changes and reminders
> - Track completed and upcoming sessions
> - Manage notification preferences
> - Secure login with your TSIN credentials
